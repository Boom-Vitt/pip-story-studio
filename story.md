# Pip and the Little Star

A gentle, all-ages bedtime story. **Runtime: 60.0 s**, 6 scenes, 138 narration words.

**Logline:** The smallest star tumbles out of the sky into a meadow. Pip, a small, slow
snail with a big heart, carries her up the tallest hill. It isn't high enough, so he
calls his firefly friends, and together they glow her home. From then on she always
twinkles a little extra for Pip.

## Characters

- **Pip**: a tiny round snail with a swirl shell. Small, slow, kind. Expressive eye
  stalks, blush cheeks, a determined little smile. Moves in soft squash-and-stretch
  inches.
- **Little Star ("Star")**: the smallest star, a soft rounded five-point star with a
  face. Sleepy and wobbly at first. Her glow is her mood: bright when happy, dim and
  grey-ish when sad.
- **Fireflies**: 8–12 round, bobbing glow-dots with tiny wings. A warm, friendly swarm.

## Full narration (138 words)

High above a sleepy meadow, the stars woke up one by one. The smallest star stretched,
and yawned, and wobbled… and tumbled all the way down — plink! — into the clover, right
next to Pip the snail. "Oh no," she whispered. "I can't shine from down here." Pip was
small, and Pip was slow, but Pip had a very big heart. "Hop on," he smiled. "I'll take
you home." Up the tallest hill they went, one tiny step at a time. But at the very top,
the sky was still so far away. So Pip called out to his friends, the fireflies. Glow by
glow, they gathered round and lifted the little star higher… and higher… all the way
home. That night she shone brighter than ever, and every night after, she twinkled a
little extra — just for Pip.

## Emotional arc

Wonder (1) → surprise and sadness (2) → warmth and resolve (3) → effort, then a low
moment (4) → hope, friends helping (5) → joy and a warm payoff (6).
